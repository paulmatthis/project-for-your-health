### How this works

It's a git repo, so any device you open this Code project on syncs first, then pushes your entries automatically. Log from your phone, laptop, wherever, it all stays in sync.

There's also a spreadsheet `project-200-tracker.xlsx` that mirrors everything with daily and weekly totals calculated automatically. It updates from your primary device.

Calorie and macro targets come from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for resting metabolic rate, scaled by activity level for total daily energy expenditure, then adjusted for goal. Protein, fat, and carb targets follow personalized, evidence-based ratios for body composition goals.

Just send what you eat, drink, or buy. Send it anytime, in any format, typed, dictated, or send images. Let me do the rest!

### Example interactions

- "How am I doing today?" or just "daily report" for totals against your targets
- "Weekly report" for the week so far and more in-depth comparison against overall goals and progress
- "What groceries should I buy today?" which can include a cost-per-macro comparison
- "Suggest a weekly meal plan with easy prep based on my habits"

Be as brief or in-depth as you like. Have fun!

#### Citations

- DIETFITS trial (Gardner CD et al., JAMA 2018): low-carb vs low-fat converge once protein/calories matched. [https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2018.0245](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2018.0245). Basis for a high-protein/moderate-carb approach instead of strict keto.
- Alcohol calorie-compensation meta-analysis (British Journal of Nutrition): about 256 kcal/day not compensated for. Basis for beer getting its own explicit budget line instead of being ignored.
- Pontzer et al. (Science 2021): flat metabolism from 20s-50s. [https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abe5017](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abe5017). Basis for treating budget, environment, and alcohol as the driver of weight gain, not "getting older."
