Itemized grocery purchases. This is different from spending-log.md, which just tracks category totals for budgeting - this one tracks actual items bought, and feeds two things: (1) meal plan suggestions from what's likely already on hand, (2) recommendations on what to buy next time to maximize calories/protein per dollar.

How to process a grocery receipt:
1. Log every food/drink line item individually below (skip non-food items like paper towels unless notable). Also log it as a lump sum in spending-log.md under Groceries as usual - the two docs serve different purposes.
2. Archive the receipt image per CLAUDE.md (images/) before or alongside logging it, so a misread line item can be checked later.
3. Don't try to build a perfect running inventory of what's left in the fridge - that's not reliable from receipts alone. Use recency as a rough proxy instead: assume most of a grocery haul gets used within 1-2 weeks, and cross-reference food-log.md to see what's already been reported eaten.

When asked for a meal plan: use recent purchases here, the targets and preferences in profile-and-targets.md, and propose meals from what's already been bought before suggesting anything new.

When asked what to buy next time: compare rough $/g protein and $/kcal across recent purchases and flag poor-value items against cheaper swaps that hit the same targets (chicken thighs over breast, canned fish, eggs, dried beans/lentils, tofu, whey powder, rice/oats/potatoes over pricier substitutes).
